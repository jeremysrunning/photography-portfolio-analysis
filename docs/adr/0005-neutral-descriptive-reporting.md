# ADR 0005: Use Neutral, Descriptive Reporting

- Status: Accepted
- Date: 2026-07-27

## Context

Photography analysis can easily drift into aesthetic scoring or prescriptive critique.

## Decision

Reports describe patterns without ranking quality.

The software must not assign aesthetic scores, judge compliance with compositional rules, or imply that one measured behavior is better than another.

## Consequences

- Findings use neutral language.
- Measurements such as subject placement replace terms such as rule-of-thirds compliance.
- Interpretations and confidence are shown explicitly.
